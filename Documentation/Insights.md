#  Page 1 – Executive Overview

## Business Performance Summary

The Olist marketplace served **96K+ unique customers** who placed **96K+ orders**, generating approximately **R$15.84M** in total revenue.

Revenue increased from **R$354 in September 2016** to a peak of **R$1.17M in November 2017**. From late 2017 onward, monthly revenue remained consistently around **R$1M**, indicating sustained business growth and a stable revenue base.

---

##  Product Category Performance

More than **70 product categories** contributed to marketplace revenue.

### Top Revenue-Generating Categories

| Category | Revenue |
|----------|---------:|
| Health & Beauty | **R$1.40M** |
| Watches & Gifts | **R$1.31M** |
| Bed Bath & Table | **R$1.24M** |

These categories consistently outperform the rest of the catalog and represent the marketplace's strongest revenue drivers.

### Recommendation

- Prioritize **Health & Beauty**, **Watches & Gifts**, and **Bed Bath & Table** for future investment.
- Expand inventory, strengthen supplier relationships, and increase promotional efforts in these categories.
- These products have demonstrated strong customer demand and should remain the primary drivers of revenue growth.

---

### Lowest Revenue-Generating Categories

| Category | Revenue |
|----------|---------:|
| Security & Services | **R$324** |
| Fashion Children's Clothes | **R$665** |
| CDs/DVDs | **R$954** |

Several additional categories contribute only a negligible portion of total revenue.

### Recommendation

- Deprioritize the bottom-performing categories unless they support a broader strategic objective such as entering a new market or improving catalog diversity.
- Reallocate marketing and inventory budgets toward higher-performing categories with proven customer demand.

---

##  Geographic Performance

São Paulo remains the marketplace's dominant state, contributing the highest share of customers and revenue.

Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais are the next strongest markets, demonstrating substantial demand while offering greater opportunity for incremental growth.

### Recommendation

- Continue supporting **São Paulo** as the company's primary revenue hub.
- Focus customer acquisition and regional marketing initiatives on **Rio de Janeiro** and **Minas Gerais**, where existing demand suggests greater room for expansion than the already mature São Paulo market.

---

#  Page 2 – Customer Analysis

## Customer Retention

Customer acquisition is strong, but long-term retention remains a major challenge.

- Only **3.12%** of customers made more than one purchase.
- More than **96%** of customers purchased only once.

This indicates that while Olist attracts new customers effectively, very few return for additional purchases.

### Recommendation

Invest in customer retention through loyalty programs, personalized recommendations, post-purchase engagement, and targeted discounts aimed at converting first-time buyers into repeat customers.

---

## RFM Segmentation

Customer segmentation highlights a significant churn risk.

| Segment | Customers |
|---------|----------:|
| At Risk / Lost | **66,182 (69%)** |
| Standard | **17,691 (18%)** |

Together, nearly **87%** of customers have never returned for another purchase.

### Recommendation

Launch re-engagement campaigns targeting At Risk and Standard customers before they permanently churn.

---

## High-Value Customers

Only **389 customers (0.4%)** qualify as **Champion Customers**, defined as customers with:

- Two or more purchases
- Lifetime spending above **R$500**

Although small in number, these customers represent the marketplace's most valuable customer segment.

### Recommendation

Reward Champion customers through loyalty programs, exclusive offers, early product access, and personalized experiences to maximize customer lifetime value.

---

## RFM Validation

The customer scatter plot reveals a natural separation around the **R$500 lifetime spend threshold**, supporting the RFM segmentation and indicating that the chosen threshold reflects genuine purchasing behavior rather than an arbitrary cutoff.

#  Page 3 – Delivery & Customer Experience

## Customer Satisfaction

The overall average review score is **4.09 / 5**.

Late deliveries receive an average review score of only **2.27 / 5**, representing a decline of nearly two full rating points.

This demonstrates that delivery reliability is one of the strongest drivers of customer satisfaction.

---

## Delivery Performance

| KPI | Value |
|-----|------:|
| On-Time Orders | **92.13%** |
| Late Orders | **6.57%** |

Although delayed deliveries account for a relatively small percentage of total orders, they have a disproportionately negative impact on customer reviews.

### Recommendation

Improving delivery reliability for the small percentage of delayed orders offers one of the highest-return operational improvements, with the potential to significantly increase customer satisfaction and marketplace reputation.

---

## Delivery Time Trends

Delivery times fluctuated considerably during the early months of the dataset due to low transaction volumes.

From 2017 onward, delivery performance stabilized within approximately **10–20 days**, indicating a mature and consistent logistics operation.

> **Note:** Early 2016 trends should be interpreted cautiously because the dataset contains relatively few orders during this period.

---

#  Key Business Recommendations

- Prioritize investment in **Health & Beauty**, **Watches & Gifts**, and **Bed Bath & Table**, as they consistently generate the highest revenue.
- Deprioritize the bottom-performing product categories unless they support a specific long-term strategic initiative.
- Continue strengthening the company's presence in **São Paulo** while focusing future expansion efforts on **Rio de Janeiro** and **Minas Gerais**, where additional growth opportunities exist.
- Customer acquisition is healthy, but increasing repeat purchases should become the organization's primary customer strategy.
- Reduce the relatively small percentage of late deliveries to significantly improve customer satisfaction and overall marketplace experience.
- Protect and nurture Champion customers through loyalty initiatives, as they represent the marketplace's highest-value customer segment.
